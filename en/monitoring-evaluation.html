Practice 01 · MEAL

Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning

Measure to decide better. We design and run monitoring and evaluation systems that turn your project data into informed decisions.

Our offer

What we do

Dashboards, digital data collection, performance analysis.

  • Dashboards
  • Digital data collection
  • Performance analysis
  • Design of M&E systems: results frameworks, indicators, M&E plans
  • Baseline studies, mid-term and final evaluations of projects and programmes
  • Digital data collection on smartphones and tablets
  • Dashboards and performance analysis
  • Accountability and complaints and feedback mechanisms (CFM), including an independent phone line
  • Knowledge capitalisation, lessons learned and organisational learning

Typical deliverables

What you receive

  • M&E plan and results measurement framework
  • Evaluation reports: baseline, mid-term, final
  • Cleaned and documented databases
  • Monitoring dashboards
  • Learning briefs and recommendations

Every deliverable is adapted to the commissioning party’s requirements and validated with your teams.

Accountability · Dedicated offer

An independent phone line so that beneficiaries can make their voices heard.

A-Z Contact Center & Data Intelligence gives projects a dedicated phone channel: beneficiaries can report a complaint, ask a question, make a suggestion or give feedback remotely. Built into the project’s complaints and feedback mechanism (CFM), it does not replace local complaints committees but complements them, turning every call into structured data to improve interventions.

  • Easy to reach
  • Simple to use
  • Potentially free for the beneficiary
  • Guaranteed confidentiality
  • Full traceability
  • Case follow-up
