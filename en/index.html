Our approach

A four-step method, one common thread.

Every assignment follows the same path: understand the context, produce reliable data, analyse it rigorously and turn it into useful decisions.

  1. 01 · Understand

    Scoping, analysis of the terms of reference, desk review and choice of indicators.

    • Terms of reference
    • Desk review
    • Indicators

  2. 02 · Collect

    Quantitative and qualitative field surveys: interviews, focus groups, digital collection on smartphones and tablets.

    • Surveys
    • Focus groups
    • Mobile collection

  3. 03 · Analyse

    Statistical processing, qualitative analysis and dashboards: reliable, readable and verifiable results.

    • Statistics
    • Qualitative analysis
    • Dashboards

  4. 04 · Act

    Feedback sessions, operational recommendations, team training and knowledge capitalisation.

    • Recommendations
    • Training
    • Learning
