About us

A Malian consulting firm, from data to action.

A-Z Sarl provides services in economic, social and cultural development in general, and in particular in project monitoring and evaluation, socio-economic studies, digital transformation and vocational training.

Who we are

A multidisciplinary team serving development projects.

Based in Hamdallaye ACI 2000, Bamako, A-Z Sarl, which signs its work “A-Z Innovation & Impact”, supports public institutions, development partners, NGOs, associations and companies from idea to impact, in Mali, across Africa and internationally.

The firm’s consultants bring the skills and expertise required in thirteen sectors, from monitoring and evaluation of projects and programmes to advisory support for farmer groups.

Our conviction: a useful decision rests on reliable data. That is why we combine methodological rigour, field presence and digital tools.

Our delivery model

Teams built for each assignment.

A-Z Sarl maintains a large pool of expert CVs, proposed according to their expertise in response to tenders.

  1. A pool of expert CVs

    Consultants, sector experts, trainers and business practitioners, available as needed.

  2. A tailored team

    For each tender, we put forward the profiles that precisely match the assignment.

  3. An assignment delivered

    The team works under the coordination of the technical management, from data collection to the final report.
