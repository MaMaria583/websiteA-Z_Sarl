Expert pool

Join our network of experts.

A-Z Sarl maintains a large pool of expert CVs, proposed according to their expertise in response to tenders. We regularly call on consultants, experts, trainers and business practitioners to build our delivery teams.

Profiles we look for

Four types of profiles, 13 sectors.

Consultants

Leading studies, evaluations and advisory assignments.

Sector experts

Health, education, governance, environment, rural development and more.

Trainers

Designing and delivering practical adult training.

Business practitioners

Professionals who share hands-on operational experience.

How it works

Three steps to an assignment.

  1. You submit your CV

    With your main field and years of experience.

  2. We review your profile

    Your CV joins our expert pool, filed by sector.

  3. We mobilise you

    As soon as a tender matches your expertise, we get in touch.
